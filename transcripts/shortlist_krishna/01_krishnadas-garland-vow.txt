# 1. Krishnadas & the 12-Year Garland Vow

Window: 28:07–31:39   |   Tight reel cut: 30:08-31:39

Summary: Devotee vows to garland Krishna's idol daily for 12 years so Krishna comes to HIS hut on year 13; heartbroken when He seems absent, a cowherd boy reveals he IS Krishna.

Why reel-worthy: Strongest tearjerker; slow build to 'main hi to hoon tumhara Krishna'. Host cries.
