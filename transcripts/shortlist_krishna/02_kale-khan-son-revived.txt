# 2. Kale Khan — Muslim Devotee's Son Revived

Window: 56:00–60:39   |   Tight reel cut: 59:19-60:39

Summary: A Muslim postman devoted to Krishna loses his son to snakebite; lays the body at the temple and challenges God, whose tear revives the child.

Why reel-worthy: Interfaith + resurrection + child's line about his 'curly-haired friend'.
