# 3. Sudama & Krishna — Feet Washed

Window: 104:40–107:21   |   Tight reel cut: 105:04-107:21

Summary: Krishna runs barefoot from his throne to embrace poor friend Sudama, weeps, washes his feet.

Why reel-worthy: Fresh 'Krishna wept only three times' fact; friendship over status.
