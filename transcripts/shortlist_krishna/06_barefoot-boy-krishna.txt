Window: 61:01–62:38   |   Tight reel cut: 61:01-62:38

Summary: A man buys sandals for a barefoot boy selling garlands; boy asks if he is Krishna, whom he'd asked for shoes.

Why reel-worthy: Feel-good takeaway: anyone can become Krishna's friend.
