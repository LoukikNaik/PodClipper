# 5. Ajab Kumari Beats God at Dice

Window: 83:39–85:26   |   Tight reel cut: 83:39-85:16

Summary: A Mewar princess plays dice daily with Shrinath Ji's idol; the stone idol grabs her arm and grants her wish (origin of Nathdwara).

Why reel-worthy: Stone idol physically grabbing her arm = goosebumps; explains a pilgrimage site.
